 High Five Opener

 This one is good for loud bars and clubs as a way to come in
 at a high energy level and take over the group while adding
 value to its members.

  Walk up to the first woman and say:

  “You’re awesome; high five.”

  Then high five her. Look at second woman and say:

  “You know what, you’re awesome too; high five.”

  Then when she goes to high five you, you make her miss
  your hand completely and say:

  “Ohhh! You fell for the oldest one in the book.”

 This routine can be used to meet additional women. If you
 talk to the first group of women for a while and you’re not
 interested in them, have them help you approach another
 group, like this:

  “I still can’t believe you fell for my high five joke! How many
  girls in here do you think would fall for that?”

  (She responds.)

  “How about that girl?” (Point out someone else.)

  (She answers.)
