Use vocal modulation: Let your voice acquire a variable rhythm and tone. Use it to express emotion. If
not, you will sound “flat” and have a difficult time retaining anyone’s interest. Again, push “too far”
in the direction of expressiveness. What at first feels like too much is often the right amount or even
still not enough.
Body Language
Good body language is relaxed, calm, and confident. Bad body language is associated with (and
usually comes from) insecurity, nervousness, and defensiveness.


As a general rule, if a position looks uncomfortable, it conveys low status. This is true whether or not
a position happens to be comfortable for you. High-status men do not tend to squish themselves into
awkward or uncomfortable positions.


Partly because of this, we endorse the principle of “locking in.”
