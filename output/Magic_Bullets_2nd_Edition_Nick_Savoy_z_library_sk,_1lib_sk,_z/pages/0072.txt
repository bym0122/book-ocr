Emotions and Sexual Behavior


Most women tend to make sexual decisions based more on their emotional state than pure physical
attraction. This does not mean that your looks are not important. They absolutely are, and if you are
good-looking, some women will have sex with you based on your looks alone. However, while women
vary greatly, most of the time you will need to make an emotional impact. One thing we’ve found in
our combined tens of thousands of approaches is that there are four emotional triggers that – if you
can activate them all – tend to make women say yes.


These are:


         Feeling that a man’s value is equal to or greater than hers.


         Feeling that she’s special to him or that she’s earned his attention.


         Feeling comfort and connection with him.


         Feeling aroused by his touch without awkwardness or embarrassment.


In general, your best chance of sleeping with a woman is to trigger these four emotions in her.
