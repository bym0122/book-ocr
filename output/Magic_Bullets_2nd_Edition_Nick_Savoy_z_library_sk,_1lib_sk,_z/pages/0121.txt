embed DHVs, not how to construct a great Attraction story. How many of these embedded DHVs did

There are two key rules to follow when embedding DHVs. First, the story must still be interesting,
even if you took out all the embedded DHVs. Second, each embedded DHV should be very relevant to
the story. Err on the side of caution; it’s better to undersell your embedded DHVs than it is to be seen
as bragging.
