Shirts


         • Keep informal collared shirts outside your pants; don’t tuck them in.


         • Wear collared shirts alone, without a t-shirt underneath. Only wear a t-shirt if you sweat a
         lot.


         • Short-sleeve button-down shirts are often boring and ugly.


         • Shirts give you a lot of room for subtlety and creativity. You should almost never wear a
         plain, solid-colored, collared shirt unless the color itself is unique. Even if for whatever
         reason you are dead set on wearing a plain white shirt, you can give it an intriguing touch
         with a bit of texture, subtle patterns, or French cuffs.
