Phone Game


Most phone game takes place in the Comfort phase (see Chapter 9). However, you need to think about
phone game differently from regular, in-person comfort building. This is partly because one of the
major purposes and challenges of being on the phone with a woman is to arrange for the two of you to
actually meet up in person again.


In addition, the nature of your interaction is different on the phone. You can’t see each other’s body
language. You can prepare much better. Neither of you actually has to answer the phone. Most people
have Caller ID. Conversations can end with very little notice. And so on. We’ll analyze the impact of
these factors in this chapter.
