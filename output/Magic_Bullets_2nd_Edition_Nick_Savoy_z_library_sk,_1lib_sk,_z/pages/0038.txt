Getting Her Phone Number


     • Make sure you get her number; don’t just give her yours.


     • You can get a phone number anytime in or after the Attraction phase, but it’s best to wait
     until Comfort. We explain why in Chapter 23 (Phone Game).


     • Make specific plans for what you two will do that is interesting and doesn’t involve dinner
     and a movie.


     • Stay for at least 5 minutes after you get her number.


     • Chapter 22 covers how to get a woman’s phone number so she’ll answer when you call and
     what to do in the first phone conversation.
