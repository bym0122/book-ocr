Phrasal Transition


A phrasal transition is really “No Transition” with crutches. The crutch is that you say something to
connect the Approaching phase and the Attraction phase like:


         • That reminds me of…


         • That’s just like when…


         • Yeah, that’s crazy, because…


You can use these even if there is no connection between the subject of your opener and what you’re
about to say next (which will be in the Attraction phase). Usually there won’t be.
