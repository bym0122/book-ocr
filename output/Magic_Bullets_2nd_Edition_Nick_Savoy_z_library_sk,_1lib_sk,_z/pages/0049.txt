Evolutionary biology for women


Just as men generally prefer wider hips, women have near-universal preferences as well. We can
divide them into three categories:


Foundations:


         Health.
         Social Intuition.
         Humor.


Attributes:


         Status.
         Wealth.


Congruence:


         Pre-selected.
         Challenging.
         Confident.


Note that these are not the only or even the necessary characteristics to win a woman’s interest. These
are simply the most important qualities that are common to most women. Every woman will also have
her unique interests when it comes to men, and most are also looking for elements which must be built
together, like trust, connection, and shared values. Nevertheless, if you know very little about a
specific woman - which will often be the case before you get to know her – these are your eight key
attraction switches. They have attracted women for generations.
