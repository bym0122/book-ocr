  Do Not Seem Too Excited
  A good rule of thumb is to double the time that you think that you should take between each call or text message. While you
  lose some text momentum by taking your time to reply, you also gain by credibly signaling non-neediness and abundance.
  Therefore, in general, for a Long Fuse, take your time in replying and vary the time that you take between replies to her text
  messages.
